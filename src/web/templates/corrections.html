Corrections & listings

Report a wrong price, a missing facility, or a listing that should change.

What to include

  • The page or facility, and the procedure or CPT code.
  • What's wrong and what it should say.
  • A public link that shows the right price (the facility's price page, PDF, or price-transparency file). We publish prices only from public sources, so a link is what lets us change it.

What happens next

Every report goes into a review queue that a person reads. Nothing is published automatically. We aim to review reports within {{ sla }}, and when a fix is confirmed it ships with the next site update. Facilities can't pay for placement, so a listing request is only about whether the price data is right.
