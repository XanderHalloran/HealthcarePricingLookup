About {{ brand.name }}

{{ brand.name }} is an independent price guide for {{ cov.states|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') or 'Arizona' }}. It turns the price files hospitals are required to publish, plus the cash price lists that independent imaging centers, labs, and clinics post on their own sites, into pages you can browse and compare.

It's also a negotiation tool. Every procedure page shows a Medicare benchmark and a suggested "ask", and if you already have a bill you can paste it on the home page to get an appeal letter and the exact numbers to cite.

Independence

There is no paid placement and no advertising inside the price lists. Nobody can pay to be listed or ranked. See trust & limits for the full rules, and methodology for how every number is built.
